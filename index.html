    Surviving the Game as a Barbarian (게임 속 바바리안으로 살아남기) is a Korean web novel written by Jung Yoon-kang (정윤강). It was first serialized by Naver Series on March 31, 2021 and is still ongoing. On April 19, 2023, a webtoon serialization was released on Naver Series, adapted by Han tae-su (한태수) and MIDNIGHT STUDIO.

    “After spending nine years on Dungeon and Stone, the role-playing game that no player has ever beaten, Hansu Lee finally reaches the dungeon of the final boss. As soon as he opens the gate, however, he suddenly sees a message that says his tutorial has been completed and finds himself in the body of Bjorn Yandel, a real barbarian within the game. An exciting adventure awaits him as he endeavors to abide by the laws of the fictional world!”
